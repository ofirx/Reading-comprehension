Listening activity

Read the text below once. After you read the text once, you can listen: press the red Play button to listen to the text. Press Pause after you hear the sentence I'm your new English teacher. Then answer the short live worksheet below. When you finish, press Play again and continue listening.

קראו את הטקסט למטה פעם אחת. אחרי שקראתם את הטקסט פעם אחת, תוכלו להאזין: לחצו על כפתור Play האדום כדי להאזין לטקסט. לחצו על Pause אחרי המשפט I'm your new English teacher. ענו על דף העבודה האינטראקטיבי הקצר למטה. אחרי שסיימתם, לחצו שוב על Play והמשיכו להאזין. 

Hello pupils,
Welcome to English class!
I am happy to be your new teacher.
We will learn, speak, read, and have fun together.
My name is Tali.
I'm your new English teacher.
It's nice to meet you.
Please write to me. I want your name, phone number and address.
English lessons are on Mondays and Thursdays. 🤓
You need a book, a notebook and a pencil. 
No food, please.
See you on Monday.
Tali 🙂
